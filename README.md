# TREASURE HUNT || ZOMBIE APOCALYPSE :zombie:

This mini-game is a treasure hunt, but set in a zombie apocalypse.

Your goal is to achieve the highest score as you explore different locations and of course, to make it out alive. :runner:

## How to play :game_die:

After writing your name, in each round you must:

- :round_pushpin: Select the location to search for
- :arrow_right: The events will depend on your choice
- :warning: Try not to die and collect the best items
- :8ball: Decide whether to keep searching
